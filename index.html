03 / OUR STORY

From cleanrooms to living rooms.
Now, to the software you run on.

Pharma plants first. Homes next.
Software today.

We didn’t start with code. We started on pharmaceutical shop floors, where a cleanroom door that opens at the wrong moment can cost a whole batch. That is where we learned to build things that simply have to work.

Since 2019 we have engineered controls for pharma cleanrooms, then carried the same discipline into smart homes, offices and hotels. Neural Genesis Private Limited is the next step: a software company that brings pharma-grade reliability to the apps businesses use every day.

  1. 012019 · INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION

    Pharma-grade believers

    Neural Industrial Automation builds controllers and instruments for cleanrooms: door interlocking systems, air shower controllers, differential pressure indicators, control panels and environmental monitoring, trusted by 200+ pharmaceutical clients.

    • Custom firmware
